# Plain-language market brief (format and tone)

Content comes from `ph-stock-market-research` and `ph-stock-verdict`. This skill only shapes how it reads.

## Core rules
- **Lead with the answer** in one bold line: the verdict or "what to do today".
- Fit **one phone screen** for alerts and add-ons, **two** for a full brief. Cut anything that doesn't change what the reader does.
- Use short sentences, everyday words, ₱ amounts and share counts rather than percentages alone.
- **Explain a term the first time** in brackets or a short clause: "P/B (what you pay for ₱1 of assets)". Use the glossary in `ph-market-playbook` §8.
- Give numbers with their time: "₱35.65 at 1:40 PM".
- Keep facts, the crowd's view and our view visibly separate.
- Say what changed since the last message. If a previous call changed, say "I've changed my mind" and why.
- Use one level of bullets, and bold only the key numbers and the action.
- End trade advice with: _This is guidance, not a guarantee._

## Section order (full brief)
1. **Bottom line:** 1–2 sentences.
2. **What moved / what's new:** 3–5 bullets, each with the reason.
3. **What this means for you:** link the news to the reader's plan or positions.
4. **What to do:** exact orders (shares, limit, max, wrong-if), by budget tier, with cash left.
5. **Watch next:** dated calendar items.
6. **Sources:** inline links.

## Short formats
| Type | Shape |
|---|---|
| Order alert / fill check | 1 bold line + 2–3 bullets + the next action |
| "Before the Bell" add-on | Title + date + 3 bullets that agree or disagree with the morning brief + link |
| "Not out yet" note | One line + link to the latest edition (dated) |
| Stock question | The `ph-stock-verdict` §7 template |

## Words to avoid → use instead
| Avoid | Say |
|---|---|
| "Accumulate on weakness" | "Buy more if it drops to ₱X" |
| "Support at X" | "It has stopped falling near ₱X before" |
| "Stop-loss" | "Rethink if it closes below ₱X" |
| "Bullish/bearish sentiment" | "Most traders on X are upbeat/worried" |
| "Ex-div" | "Own it by the close on <date> to get the dividend" |
| Unexplained tickers in lists | "AREIT (Ayala's office REIT)" the first time |

## Don'ts
- No promises of returns, no hype, no emoji clutter.
- Don't present an unconfirmed dividend or date as fact.
- Don't send wall-of-text tables in chat. Use a Page (read the `canvas` skill) for long tables.
- Don't mention missing internal sources unless it changes the advice.
